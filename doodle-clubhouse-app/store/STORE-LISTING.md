## Description
Welcome to the Doodle Clubhouse: a cosy, colourful place to draw, doodle and make cartoons. Simple enough for kids, fun for everyone.

DRAW ANYTHING
• Pencil, spray paint, paint bucket, eraser, straight lines and shapes
• Bright crayons, a colour mixer and an eyedropper
• Stamps: hearts, stars, flowers, a UFO with a little alien, planets, butterflies and more, in any colour
• Train tracks: draw a track and a little train rides along it
• Type words in 7 fun fonts, then move and turn them
• Plain, notebook and chalkboard paper
• Pinch to zoom in for tiny details
• Works great with Apple Pencil

BOOM! DYNAMITE ERASER
Plant a stick of cartoon dynamite, watch the fuse fizz… and poof! A clean page. (Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.)

MAKE CARTOONS
• Frame-by-frame flipbook animation
• Draw a background once and it stays behind every frame
• Ghosts of the frame before and after help you line things up
• Bounce back and forth, drag frames into a new order
• Move a whole drawing, add frames anywhere
• Record your voice and it plays with your cartoon (and goes into the video)
• Play your cartoon medium, fast or super fast

SPEED ART
Every stroke is recorded. Watch your whole drawing come together in a fast video, and save it to share.

SAVE & SHARE
• Save drawings as pictures, and animations as GIFs or videos
• Share to Photos, Messages, YouTube and more

MAKE IT YOURS
Change the colours of the menu bar, tool bar and background.

BRUSH PACK (OPTIONAL, ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
Six special brushes (Paintbrush, Rainbow, Pixel, Fur, Chalk and Mirror), four magic effects (Wormhole, Black light, Tornado and Flush), unlimited animation frames and no watermark. One purchase, yours to keep, and it works with Family Sharing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads
• No accounts or sign-in
• No tracking or data collection
• Everything stays on your device

## Notes for App Review
Doodle Clubhouse is an all-ages drawing and animation app with no accounts, ads, analytics or network features. The optional Brush Pack is a one-time in-app purchase (product ID brush_pack): tap any locked brush to see it, and "Restore purchase" is on the same screen. The "Dynamite" tool is a playful cartoon eraser that clears the canvas; undo restores it. The "Flush" effect is a cartoon toilet that flushes the drawing away (also undoable).
