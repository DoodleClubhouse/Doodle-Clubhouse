## Description
Welcome to the Doodle Clubhouse, a cosy, colourful place for kids to draw, doodle and make their very first cartoons.

DRAW ANYTHING
• Pencil, soft airbrush, spray paint, straight lines and eraser
• 10 bright crayons, a colour mixer and an eyedropper
• Stickers: stars, hearts, flowers and smileys
• Three papers: plain, lined notebook and chalkboard
• Pinch to zoom in for tiny details

BOOM! DYNAMITE ERASER
Plant a stick of cartoon dynamite, watch the fuse fizz… and poof! A clean page. (Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.)

MAKE CARTOONS
• Frame-by-frame flipbook animation that's simple enough for little hands
• A "ghost" of the last frame helps you line things up
• Play your cartoon slow, medium or fast

SAVE & SHARE
• Save drawings as pictures, and animations as GIFs or videos
• Pin your favourites to your own Wall of fame

BRUSH PACK (OPTIONAL, ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
Six special brushes (Paintbrush, Rainbow, Pixel, Fur, Chalk and Mirror) plus the paint bucket and unlimited animation frames. Purchases are behind a grown-up check and work with Family Sharing.

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads, ever
• No accounts or sign-in
• No tracking or data collection
• Everything stays on your device
