Doodle Clubhouse

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 8, 2026

Doodle Clubhouse is a drawing and animation app for kids and families. We built it so children can create freely and privately.

We don't collect any information

Doodle Clubhouse does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Your drawings stay on your device

Everything you draw, your animations, your "Wall of fame" and your settings are saved only on your own iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Saving and sharing

When you tap Save or Share, the app opens Apple's share sheet so you can choose where your picture or video goes (for example, Photos or Messages). The app only does this when you ask it to.

Purchases

The optional Brush Pack is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. A grown-up has to answer a question before the purchase screen appears. We never see or receive any payment details.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to share, sell or disclose. This app is designed to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category rules.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
